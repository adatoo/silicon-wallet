Silicon Wallet

Private, encrypted secrets for Mac, iPad and iPhone.

Silicon Wallet keeps your passwords, cards, bank details, licence keys and notes in one vault, encrypted on your device and synced through your own iCloud account. No account, no tracking, free and open source.

  • Groups with their own fields, favourites, notes and attachments
  • Face ID and Touch ID, auto-lock, clipboard clearing and a password generator
  • End-to-end encrypted iCloud sync
  • Import from Acrylic Wallet
