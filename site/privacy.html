Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

The short version

Silicon Wallet collects no data. We can't see your vault, and nor can Apple.

What stays on your device

Your vault is stored on your device, encrypted with AES-256-GCM under a random key. That key is itself protected by a key derived from your master password with Argon2id. Your master password is never stored. The only unencrypted vault data is what's needed to open it: the encryption parameters, record identifiers, modification dates and sizes.

iCloud sync

If your device is signed in to iCloud, the encrypted vault syncs through your own private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit. Only encrypted data leaves your device. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

Face ID and Touch ID

If you turn them on, your vault key is kept in this device's Keychain, protected by Face ID or Touch ID. It never leaves the device and isn't synced. Biometric data is handled entirely by Apple and is never available to the app.

No analytics, no tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and makes no network requests apart from iCloud sync. Crash reports are sent only if you've chosen to share them with developers in your device's settings; they're collected by Apple and contain no vault data.
