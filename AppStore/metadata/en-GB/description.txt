Silicon Wallet keeps your passwords, cards, bank details, licence keys and private notes in one encrypted vault, on your Mac, iPad and iPhone.

YOUR VAULT, ENCRYPTED ON YOUR DEVICE
Everything is encrypted before it's saved or synced, with a key made from your master password. Apple can't read it, and neither can anyone else. There's no account to create and nothing is collected.

ORGANISED YOUR WAY
• Groups with their own fields: give "Wi-Fi" a network and password, or "Membership" a number and renewal date
• Favourites, notes and extra fields on any entry
• Fast search that ignores case and accents, and never looks inside hidden fields
• Attachments such as scans and PDFs, each encrypted with its own key

SAFE TO USE EVERY DAY
• Unlock with Face ID or Touch ID
• Locks itself after a few minutes, when your Mac sleeps, and when you leave the app
• Hidden fields stay hidden until you reveal them
• Copied passwords clear themselves from the clipboard
• A generator for strong passwords and easy-to-type passphrases
• An access log of unlocks and failed attempts on each device

ON ALL YOUR DEVICES
Your vault syncs through your own iCloud account, end-to-end encrypted. Change an entry on your Mac and it's on your iPhone moments later.

MOVING FROM ACRYLIC WALLET
Silicon Wallet reads Acrylic Wallet's vault file directly: groups, field labels, entries, notes, favourites and attachments all come across. You don't need the old app installed.

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
Silicon Wallet is free, with no ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases, and its source is open under the MIT Licence.
